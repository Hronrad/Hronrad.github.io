# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors to Hronrad's personal site, including potential research collaborators, open-source contributors, students, and people interested in Hronrad's technical and creative work. They use the site to understand Hronrad's interests, explore projects and research, and find ways to connect.

## Product Purpose

Present Hronrad's identity, ideas, deployed applications, open-source work, research, media, and experiments in one bilingual portfolio. Success means visitors can quickly distinguish the different bodies of work, open the relevant project or application, and recognize a coherent personal point of view across the site.

## Brand Personality

Experimental, technical, and reflective. The site should feel like a personal digital laboratory: intellectually curious, visually deliberate, and comfortable moving between research, software, art, and philosophy.

## Anti-references

Do not turn the site into a conventional SaaS landing page, a generic developer portfolio, or a neutral academic profile. Avoid introducing card styles, typography, colors, or interaction patterns that compete with the established pixel interface and its alternate glass/collage mode.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the site's dual identity: every addition must belong naturally to both the pixel and glass modes.
2. Make real work directly explorable through clear project names, concise descriptions, and explicit destination links.
3. Keep technical depth legible without flattening the site's experimental personality.
4. Treat Chinese and English as equal first-class experiences.
5. Extend existing components and interaction patterns before introducing new visual vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic headings, descriptive link labels, keyboard-accessible controls, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and meaningful bilingual copy. Preserve content access when motion is reduced or visual effects are unavailable.
